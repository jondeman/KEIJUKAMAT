## 6. Creative Opportunity Zones

### opp_01: Modernization of Expertise ('Digital Lajimestari')
*Risk: Medium* 🟡

**Rationale:** Transforms silent in-store knowledge into dynamic video content (tips, Q&A, problem-solving) to meet customers where they search (Google/YouTube/TikTok).

### opp_02: Honest Finnish Sports Reality ('Localized Heart of Sport')
*Risk: Low* 🟢

**Rationale:** Owning the realities of Finnish exercise (slush, darkness) creates relatability and emotional connection, differentiating from global 'glossy' brands.
